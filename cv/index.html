Summary Computer scientist and NLP researcher specializing in natural language processing for low-resource languages, computational social science, and cross-cultural AI. Co-founder of HausaNLP and Arewa Data Science, with 10+ years of experience in teaching, research, and interdisciplinary collaboration across Africa, Asia, Europe, and the Americas. Published 40+ peer-reviewed papers, several recognized with best paper and best resource paper awards, and secured over $800,000 in competitive research grants.

Publications

  • 2026
    POLAR: A Benchmark for Multilingual, Multicultural, and Multi-Event Online Polarization
    Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2026
    A benchmark of 23,000+ instances spanning 22 languages for detecting political, racial, religious, and gender-based polarization in online text.
  • 2026
    Going PLACES: Participatory Localized Red Teaming for Text-to-Image Safety in the Global South
    Proceedings of the 2026 ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT '26)
    Community-centered red-teaming study of text-to-image model safety across Ghana, Nigeria, and India, producing a dataset of 26,000+ examples of model failures.
  • 2026
    Beyond Majority Voting: Agreement-Based Clustering to Model Annotator Perspectives in Subjective NLP Tasks
    Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics
    Agreement-based clustering approach for modeling annotator perspectives across 40 datasets in 18 languages, evaluated on sentiment, emotion, and hate speech tasks.
  • 2025
    AfriHate: A Multilingual Collection of Hate Speech and Abusive Language Datasets for African Languages
    Proceedings of NAACL 2025
    The first labelled hate speech dataset for 18 African languages from 6 countries, with baseline benchmarks.
  • 2025
    BRIGHTER: BRIdging the Gap in Human-Annotated Textual Emotion Recognition Datasets for 28 Languages
    Proceedings of the 63rd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics
    A large, high-quality human-annotated emotion dataset for 28 languages (18 African). Best Resource Paper Award.
  • 2024
    Analyzing Cultural Representations of Emotions in LLMs Through Mixed Emotion Survey
    2024 12th International Conference on Affective Computing and Intelligent Interaction (ACII)
    Studied emotion representations of Western and East Asian cultures across five LLMs against a prior human study. Best Paper Award.
  • 2024
    No Culture Left Behind: ArtELingo-28, a Benchmark of WikiArt with Captions in 28 Languages
    Proceedings of the 2024 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing
    200K emotion labels and affective captions in 25 languages on 2,000 images, benchmarking Vision-Language models for cross-lingual emotion transfer.
  • 2020
    Movie Revenue Prediction Based on Purchase Intention Mining Using YouTube Trailer Reviews
    Information Processing & Management
    PhD dissertation research: box-office revenue prediction using a purchase intention lexicon mined from movie trailer reviews.
